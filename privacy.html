Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

SEP22 Games Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Sticker Books for Kids app.

1. About Sticker Books for Kids

Sticker Books for Kids is an educational app designed for toddlers and preschool children. The app provides simple learning activities involving topics such as animals, fruits, vehicles, shapes, alphabet and vocabulary.

2. Information We Collect

Sticker Books for Kids does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as a user's name, address or phone number.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly from children through the app.

3. Purchases

The app may offer an optional Premium in-app purchase through Apple's App Store.

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your credit card number, payment card details or other financial account information.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that are necessary to provide app functionality, distribution, purchases or other services.

Any third-party service used by the app is responsible for handling information according to its own privacy policy and applicable requirements.

5. Children's Privacy

Sticker Books for Kids is designed for young children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of the app and to contact us if they have questions about privacy or information handling.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the app. However, no electronic service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not require users to create an account or directly submit personal information through the app, we do not maintain a user account database for the app.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us and you would like to request its deletion, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
